NARIEL

Belgian dark dubstep and bass music. Dubstep, trap, UK garage, and industrial electronic music in stereo and Dolby Atmos.

About

NARIEL is the bass music project of Belgian producer Jordi Corten. Built on more than a decade of electronic music production and over 2 million streams under the previous alias HOWL, the project creates high-tension bass music defined by precision, atmosphere, and impact. Releases under HOWL have appeared on NCS, Tribal Trap, and Most Addictive.

Every track is built with intention. Heavy low-end, cinematic tension, and deliberate sound design replace unnecessary complexity, creating music that feels like a universe of its own: physical, immersive, and unmistakable. Rather than following trends, NARIEL focuses on records that stay compelling long after release.

Debut album: SUBJECT: N4R-13-L

A 13-track concept record produced in stereo and Dolby Atmos. Presented in spatial audio at Vuurdoop and Immersive Music Day in Hasselt (2026) for an audience that included Hans-Martin Buff, Wilfried Van Baelen, and teams from Dolby, Neumann, Sennheiser, Amptec, and VLAIO.

What makes NARIEL different

NARIEL is one of the few Belgian producers pushing trap and dubstep in a scene that runs on house and techno. Every production is built around deliberate choices rather than excess, using space, tension, and sound design as compositional tools. The result is music that hits hard without becoming cluttered. Dolby Atmos sits inside the writing process from the first idea, so silence and movement carry as much weight as the sound itself.

NARIEL creates high-tension bass music influenced by dubstep, trap, UK garage, and industrial electronic music, typically between 140 and 150 BPM.
